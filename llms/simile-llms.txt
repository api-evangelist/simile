# Simile

> Simile is a simulation platform for human behavior. Its AI-driven simulations show how and why customers, employees, or populations respond to change, letting enterprise teams find their audience, reach niche populations, de-risk decisions, segment sentiment, and test concepts before a real-world launch. Simile is building a foundation model that predicts human behavior in any situation and a product that deploys it at scale.
